GOOD TO KNOW

When can I check in and out?

Check-in is from 3 pm to 11 pm. Check-out is by 11 am. Arrival instructions are shared after booking.

Who can book?

This property is registered as a foreign tourist urban homestay and welcomes foreign tourists. Stays solely for Korean nationals are not accepted. Please confirm eligibility before booking.

Where can I check prices and availability?

Choose your dates and guest count below. Rates vary by date and length of stay. The host will confirm availability, applicable discounts, the full price, payment method and cancellation terms before you pay.

Can I cook and do laundry?

A kitchen and washing machine are available. Please clean and put away the cookware and dishes after use.

Are pets or smoking allowed?

Pets are not allowed. Smoking is prohibited indoors and on the balcony. Please respect the residential building by keeping noise low and avoiding parties.
